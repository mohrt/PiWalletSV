# Purchase a complete kit

!!! info "Coming soon"
    Pre-assembled PiWalletSV kits are not yet available.

    When ready, a complete kit will include a Raspberry Pi Zero, heat
    sink, Adafruit 240×240 TFT bonnet, ArduCam OV5647 camera module, a
    pre-flashed microSD card with signed PiWalletSV firmware, a 5 V USB
    power adapter, micro-USB power cable, a micro-USB OTG adapter
    (USB flash-drive backup before SD re-flash — not for PC connection),
    and a printed case.

    **Before you fund the device**, verify the card:
    [re-flash from a verified image](user-manual.md#verify-sd-card-on-arrival)
    before first boot (recommended).

    A **printable quick-start card** for the box is in
    [`docs/print/kit-insert.md`](print/kit-insert.md).

    In the meantime, build your own using the
    [pre-built image](download.md) or
    [from source](getting-started.md).

    Questions about kits? Message [@PiWalletSV on X](https://x.com/PiWalletSV).

    Official PiWalletSV kits and resale of printed case goods require
    prior written permission from the project (see
    [Disclaimer §12](disclaimer.md#12-kits-and-case-no-unauthorized-resale)).
