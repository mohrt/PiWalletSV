Welcome to PiWalletSV

Your air-gapped Bitcoin SV cold wallet —
keys on the Pi, chain on your phone.

Thank you for supporting an open, non-custodial signer built from off-the-shelf parts you can inspect and replace. This little computer holds your signing keys and never connects to Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, or Ethernet. Your phone or laptop runs the companion app to watch the chain and build transactions — the Pi only signs what you approve on its screen.

Be patient at boot

First power-on can take 1–2 minutes before the logo appears — the display may stay dark while the system starts. Every boot after setup is faster. Don't panic; let it finish.

🖥

Pi Zero W inside

Your kit uses a Raspberry Pi Zero W — the only Pi model we can source in meaningful quantity right now. It is slower than a desktop, but more than enough for signing and cold storage. Speed was never the goal; isolation was.

🔑

You are the backup

Write your 12- or 24-word seed phrase on paper or steel. No company can recover lost seeds or PINs. Your funds always remain recoverable in any BIP39 wallet — PiWalletSV is not required.

Beta software — read before funding

PiWalletSV is pre-release software. There is no warranty and no custodial recovery service.

Begin with testnet and small amounts until you trust the full flow. The bonnet walks you through a disclaimer on first boot; the full text is at piwalletsv.com/disclaimer

Before you fund — verify the microSD (strongly recommended) Re-flash from a GPG-verified download at piwalletsv.com/download before creating a wallet. That is the simplest way to trust the firmware. Do not boot the Pi only to "check" the card — first boot already changes it.

First boot on the bonnet

Plug the included power cable into the right-most micro-USB port, then wait 1–2 minutes for the logo.

  1. Disclaimer — read all pages; hold A to accept.
  2. Vault PIN — 4–12 digits; use a long PIN for cold storage.
  3. New or restore wallet — write every seed word offline.
  4. Airgap check — Settings → Maintenance → Airgap status.
  5. Pair companion — scan the QR at app.piwalletsv.com.
Wrong PIN 6× in a row wipes the vault on the device. Your seed phrase is the real backup — not the PIN, not support.

Controls & daily use

  • Joystick — move / change digits · A confirm · B back
  • B hold ~5 s on boot logo — factory diagnostics
  • From Wallets, press B → Settings
  • Receive — companion shows address; confirm on Pi for large amounts.
  • Send — companion builds tx → Pi scans QR → review → hold A → companion broadcasts.
  • Backup — your OTG adapter + USB stick (not included), left port → Settings → Maintenance → USB backup.

Swap or re-flash microSD

For verify, upgrade, or card replacement. Back up vault to USB or confirm you have your seed phrase first.

  1. Power off — unplug the power cable and wait a few seconds.
  2. Open the case — remove corner screws. Save the two button caps from the front lid — do not lose them.
  3. Remove the bonnet — remove its two screws, then gently pry up on either side (toward the top), a little at a time.
  4. Free the Pi board — remove the two Pi PCB screws and lift gently. The camera is on the back — do not pull hard or bend the ribbon cable.
  5. Remove the microSD — pull the card straight out (no spring latch — it simply pushes in and pulls out).
  6. On your computer — use a USB card reader; verify and re-flash from piwalletsv.com/download if needed.
  7. Reassemble — insert the card, seat the Pi and replace its screws, then seat the bonnet and replace its screws.
  8. Seat the front lid — center over the display, joystick, and A/B buttons; replace the button caps if they came out.
  9. Test before tightening — with corner screws still loose, press A, B, and the joystick; all must click freely. Then tighten the corner screws.

Assembly

Your kit ships fully assembled. Use these steps only if you took it apart for an upgrade or repair.

  1. Pi off. Press the heat sink onto the main chip.
  2. Seat the bonnet straight on the 40-pin header and replace its two screws.
  3. Connect the camera ribbon to the CSI port (latch open → insert → latch closed).
  4. Insert the microSD (label side as printed on the Pi).
  5. Plug power into the right-most micro-USB port only.
USB ports Right-most — power only (included cable). Never plug a flash drive here. Left — vault backup with your own OTG adapter + USB stick (not included).
